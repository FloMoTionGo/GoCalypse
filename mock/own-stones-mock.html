Static mock of the own-stone markers. When an armed item needs one of your own stones as its target (Lantern Ward, Turn the Lantern, Ferry, Skiff), each of your stones gets the last-move ember, enlarged to 9x9, in its centre. Before this change they only had an amber rim. You play black + gray. The other players' stones stay unmarked, even where they look like yours (the black stone at 6-6). The small ember at 5-5 is the real last move, which is yours too, so there the big ember covers it. Sample data only, no server. ?frame=0..3 freezes the flicker.

Lantern Ward: pick one of your stones, each marked with an ember -- right click to cancel.
