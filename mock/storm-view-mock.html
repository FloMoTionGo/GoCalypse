Static mock of the storm's grey (findings Phase 2a). The same board, calm on the left and in a storm's grey on the right. You play black + gray, with Lantern Ward armed. Every two seconds a stone of each kind lands, flips, is captured and is blown away (Gust), so you can watch stones in flight. Under the grey:

  • stones in flight are grey too (B7), and so are their shadows: a transparent stone's ring-shaped shadow would give it away;
  • the owner marks on the ward (3-3), the lily pad (8-2) and the seed (10-8) are hidden, and the timers stay (B8);
  • the armed Lantern Ward marks none of your stones with embers (B8);
  • no firefly halos wander around without their fireflies (B10).

Recall (B6) and the hover over a rival's mist (B9) live in main.js. client-browser.mjs checks them. Sample data only, no server. ?t=0.12 freezes the animations that many seconds into each two-second loop.
