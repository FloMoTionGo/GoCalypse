Welcome to GoCalypse

Four players, one board, a night of lanterns by the river.

How to play

Four players take turns placing stones. A group whose last free neighbouring point is filled gets captured.

  • Left click: a solid stone in your color. It fights black against white.
  • Right click: a grey stone with your pattern. It fights dots against stripes.

On the other front a stone is just a wall: it blocks, but can't be captured there. Players who share your color, or your pattern, fight on your side.
