How to play

Four players take turns placing stones. A group whose last free neighbouring point is filled gets captured.

  • Left click: your black or white stone. It fights black against white.
  • Right click: your gray or transparent stone. It fights gray against transparent.

On the other front a stone is just a wall: it blocks, but can't be captured there. Players who hold your black or white, or your gray or transparent, fight on your side.

Ending the game & scoring

On your turn you can Pass (button in the header) instead of placing a stone. When all four players pass in a row the game ends. A stone or an item played in between starts the count again.

The board is then scored the normal Go way, once on each front: every stone counts one point for its side, and so does every empty point that only that side's stones surround. A stone that is a wall on a front counts for no one there. Nothing is removed first, so capture what should go before you pass.

Reading the board

Anything that doesn't last shows a small number: the rounds it has left (a lily pad, a ward, driftwood or a fire; the number turns amber on its last round). A lily pad or ward also carries its owner's two stones, so you can see whose it is. The boat drifting on the river is only for orientation: its sign shows the turn being played.

Game over

A player's score is the lower of their two totals; the higher one only breaks ties. Players on the same side share its total.
