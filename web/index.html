How to play

Four players take turns placing stones. A group whose last free neighbouring point is filled gets captured.

  • Left click: your black or white stone. It fights black against white.
  • Right click: your gray or transparent stone. It fights gray against transparent.

Any stone can capture any other: a free point is filled whatever colour fills it, even your own stone of your other kind. The colour only decides who connects: a stone joins neighbours of its own colour (black with black, gray with gray). Players who hold your black or white, or your gray or transparent, connect with those stones and fight on your side.

Ending the game & scoring

On your turn you can Pass (button in the header) instead of placing a stone. When all four players pass in a row the game ends. A stone or an item played in between starts the count again.

Reading the board

Anything that doesn't last shows a small number: the rounds it has left (a lily pad, a ward, driftwood or a fire; the number turns amber on its last round). A lily pad or ward also carries its owner's two stones, so you can see whose it is. A seed grows in three pictures, one round each: a seed, a sprout, then a sapling. Anyone may trample the seed, but once it sprouts only its planter may play there. The boat drifting on the river is only for orientation: its sign shows the round being played (R3 = round 3).
