How to play

  1. Surround to capture

    Fill a group's last free point and it is captured.

    • Left click
      black against white
    • Right click
      gray against transparent
    Pick a stone each move

  3. Four passes end it

    You can Pass (header button) instead of placing a stone. When all four players pass in a row, the game ends.

Two fronts
Two fights at once

Black fights white, gray fights transparent. Every stone stands on one of the two fronts.

Anything can capture

A free point is filled whatever colour fills it, even your own stone of your other kind.

Colour connects

A stone only joins neighbours of its own colour: black with black, gray with gray.

Shared colour, shared side

Players who hold your black or white, or your gray or transparent, connect with those stones and fight on your side.

Reading the board
Rounds left

Anything that doesn't last shows a small number: a lily pad, a ward, driftwood or a fire. It turns amber on its last round.

Whose is it?

A lily pad or ward also carries its owner's two stones.

Seed, sprout, sapling

One picture a round. Anyone may trample the seed, but once it sprouts only its planter may play there.

The boat

It drifts on the river only for orientation: its sign shows the round being played (R3 = round 3).

    Game over

    A player's score is the lower of their two totals; the higher one only breaks ties. Territory is shared with anyone who also holds your side, but prisoners are counted per player, and never more prisoners than territory: on a front with 4 points of ground, at most 4 count. Stones killed with a stone of the other front are smothered: off the board, but no prisoners.
