Weather

A thunderstorm: 10 s of cloud and rain, up to three bolts, and a fire on every point they hit. A much weaker storm then lingers over the river for 3 rounds; the board gets a lighter share of it and every stone turns the same grey until it ends.

Item cards

What a bought item becomes: a portrait card that flies from its Night Market stall into the player's hand under the board. The frame and the gems give the tier (I teal, II amber, III cream). The face is itemCard() in sprites.js; the name is page text in the panel under the art (in the game the description goes under the dotted rule, read when the card is shown enlarged on hover). Every item, cheap to dear within each tier, then an empty place in the hand.
